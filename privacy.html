Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-18

This website

oxtail.dev is a static site. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics. Everything it needs is in the page itself: no fonts, scripts or images are loaded from other servers, and the page is set to refuse them. The animation runs on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, time and page requested, to keep the site running and secure. We don't use them to identify or follow you.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message to reply to it. It is delivered through our email providers. We don't share it or add you to a list.

Our apps

Each app we publish has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store listing and from this page once it ships. Where an app stores data on your device or in your iCloud account, that data stays yours; we do not sell personal data.
